# Natec Development Ltd.

Established in 2023, Natec Development Ltd. is a full-cycle real estate development company based in Sreepur, Gazipur, Bangladesh.

We partner with property owners and communities to deliver sustainable, well-planned developments from concept to completion.

---

### What We Do
* **Land Acquisition & Planning:** Identifying strategic locations and feasibility analysis.
* **Design & Architecture:** Modern building design tailored to community needs.
* **Construction Management:** End-to-end site oversight and quality control.
* **Sales & Marketing:** Strategic market placement and client outreach.
* **Property Management:** Long-term asset care and tenant services.
